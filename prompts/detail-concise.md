STRICT LENGTH LIMIT: the note must not exceed 60 words, and must use at most five short sentences in total - fewer paragraphs than the style above asks for is correct at this length. Keep only the clinically material facts - presenting complaint, key findings, diagnosis and plan - each stated once. Omit conversational detail, incidental narration, and repetition. Never pad, and never drop a stated diagnosis, medication or safety-netting instruction. A note longer than 60 words is a failure.
